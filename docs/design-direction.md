# Landing-page design direction

## Constraints

Follow the assessment over live-site differences: dark teal #123A48, teal #1B5063, lime #9DC31B, light teal #CCE5ED, Raleway throughout, 8px button corners, simple line icons, full-width page, and no em dashes in page copy. Product name stays Rental Property Investment Calculator. Use Intrigue or Intrigue Real Estate & Property Management for company references.

## Chosen composition

```text
White header: genuine teal wordmark | section navigation | conversation
Dark teal hero: local investor headline and actions | residential photo
Quiet reassurance strip
White how-it-works section: three ordered, separated steps
Light calculator introduction: explicit preview | sample inputs and purpose
Dark teal contact section: optional conversation | white demo form
White footer
```

Desktop uses a split hero with a real porch/bungalow photograph from the official site. Phone layouts lead with readable copy and actions, followed by a compact photo. The dark teal field is the page's strongest brand gesture; the rest stays restrained. No fabricated numerical hero stats, stock dashboard charts, testimonials, or investment guarantees.

Typography uses Raleway 400/500/600/700, sentence-case labels, left-aligned text, and short reading measures. Lime is reserved for the hero's main action. Most content remains unboxed; only the preview and form need contained surfaces.

## Review against generic defaults

Avoided a generic SaaS dashboard hero and repeated rounded feature-card grid. The photo and local investor language make the opening specific to residential investing. Ordered numbers are used only for the actual three-step process. No decorative animation, emoji icons, pill buttons, or invented social proof.

## Copy and scope

The user-supplied original brand/copy foundation guides the page. Hero headline and key actions are retained. Because this phase excludes a working calculator, explanatory copy uses future tense where necessary, and both the section label and body clearly mark the preview. No placeholder returns or chart are presented as calculated results.

Browser-only form confirmation states that nothing was sent. A distinct main-website contact link is available for real inquiries.

Official references reviewed: https://www.intriguepm.com/ and https://www.intriguepm.com/investors/. Their service-oriented tone is transferable; their pill buttons and unrelated marketing claims are not automatically transferable.
